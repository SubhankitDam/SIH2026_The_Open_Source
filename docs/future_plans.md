## Future Plans:
* Permission from the government to use a person's official government ID or some other ID card specifically for our website for stronger authorization


* If a user has already logged in on our website and has met an unfortunate accident,
it might happen that their family or relatives are having difficulty in obtaining their ID.
In that case they can use an emergency number assigned by the patient themselves


* **Medical SOS on phones** - users can enter their medical info.
Additionally, the patient's ID will also be in it after government's permission.<br>
  Either this ID can be used, or a government ID proof can be used by anyone
to get the patient's ID temporarily. This will be applicable only during emergency sessions.
